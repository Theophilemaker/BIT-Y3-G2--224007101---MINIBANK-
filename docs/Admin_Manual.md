## 2. Managing Customers
1. Go to **Customers** on the sidebar.
2. Click **Add Customer** to manually create an account for someone.
3. Click the **Edit (pencil)** icon to change details or suspend a user.
4. Click the **Delete (trash)** icon to remove a user permanently.

## 3. Managing Accounts
1. Go to **Accounts** on the sidebar.
2. Click the **Freeze (lock)** icon to suspend a specific account.
3. Click the **Delete (trash)** icon to remove an account. 
   *Note: The system will block deletion if the account has a balance greater than zero.*

## 4. System Settings
1. Go to **System Settings**.
2. Here you can change the Site Name, Slogan, and Logo URL.
3. Click **Save Changes**. The entire website updates instantly.

## 5. Viewing Contact Messages
1. Go to **Contact Messages** on the sidebar.
2. You will see all messages sent by the public.
3. Click the **Delete** icon to remove spam or handled messages.
